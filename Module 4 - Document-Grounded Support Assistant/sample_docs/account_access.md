# Demo account access guide

To reset a password, select **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and use the emailed reset link within 30 minutes.

To replace a multi-factor authentication phone, open **Account > Security** and select **Replace authenticator** after signing in. If you cannot sign in, contact a support agent for identity verification.

To invite a teammate, open **Workspace > Members**, choose **Invite member**, and enter their email address. Only workspace owners and admins can invite members.
